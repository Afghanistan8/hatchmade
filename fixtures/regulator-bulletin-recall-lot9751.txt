REGENCY AEROSPACE - SERVICE BULLETIN 2026-77 (A42X)
Product line: Aero-Puck outbound assembly, P/N AP-1150.

RECALL NOTICE. Recall ACP-2026-11 is ACTIVE for this product.
Affected lot range: LOT-9720 through LOT-9782 inclusive.
Every unit of P/N AP-1150 with a lot label inside that range, including
LOT-9751, is subject to quarantine on receipt and to mandatory inspection
and retrofit before further flight assembly. Disposition of affected units
is reserved to the Regency Aerospace recall desk.

This bulletin supersedes bulletin 2026-61 and remains active until
withdrawn by a later regency service bulletin.
